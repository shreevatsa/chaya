  The issue is in the loadFiles and loadChayaFile methods. The progress bar is updated manually at different stages of the loading process.

  Here's the breakdown of what happens in loadFiles:

   1. updateLoadingProgress(0, ...): Sets the progress to 0%.
   2. updateLoadingProgress(10, ...): Sets the progress to 10% after reading the PDF file.
   3. updateLoadingProgress(20, ...): Sets the progress to 20% after initializing PDF.js.
   4. updateLoadingProgress(30, ...): Sets the progress to 30% after loading annotations.

  And in loadChayaFile:

   1. updateLoadingProgress(0, ...): Sets the progress to 0%.
   2. updateLoadingProgress(20, ...): Sets the progress to 20% after reading the ZIP.
   3. updateLoadingProgress(40, ...): Sets the progress to 40% after extracting files.
   4. updateLoadingProgress(60, ...): Sets the progress to 60% after reading the manifest.
   5. updateLoadingProgress(80, ...): Sets the progress to 80% after extracting the PDF.
   6. updateLoadingProgress(90, ...): Sets the progress to 90% after loading annotations.
   7. updateLoadingProgress(95, ...): Sets the progress to 95% after initializing the document.
   8. updateLoadingProgress(100, ...): Sets the progress to 100% when complete.

  The problem is that after these manual updates, the code calls notifyTabsDataReady(), which then triggers the rendering process in the active tab (either "Mark" or "Read"). The rendering process also updates the
  progress bar, but it does so based on the number of pages rendered.

  So, the sequence of events is:

   1. loadFiles (or loadChayaFile) sets the progress to a certain percentage (e.g., 30% or 95%).
   2. The rendering process in annotator.ts or viewer.ts starts, and it begins updating the progress bar from 0% up to 100% as it renders the pages.
